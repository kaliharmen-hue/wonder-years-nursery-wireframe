Privacy and data protection

Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 2026

The Wonder Years Day Nursery Limited is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information lawfully, fairly and securely.

This notice explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information relating to children attending or applying to attend the nursery, parents and carers, people who contact us or use our website, and visitors to the nursery.

2. The personal information we collect

Information about children

We may collect:

  • name, address and date of birth;
  • emergency contact information;
  • attendance records, booked sessions, absences and reasons for absence;
  • information about development, learning and progress;
  • observations, assessments and progress reports;
  • health, medication, immunisation, allergy and dietary information;
  • information about disabilities, special educational needs or additional support;
  • ethnicity, language, nationality and country of birth where required or relevant;
  • safeguarding information, including court orders and professional involvement;
  • information required to administer funded childcare;
  • photographs and videos where appropriate permissions or another lawful basis applies;
  • accident, incident and medication records;
  • information about previous and future education or childcare settings; and
  • other information needed to provide safe and appropriate care.

Information about parents, carers and authorised collectors

We may collect names, addresses and contact details, relationship to the child, emergency contacts, information used to confirm that a person is authorised to collect a child, payment and funding information, correspondence, permissions and information needed to administer a childcare contract.

Information collected through our website

When you contact us through the website, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, your child’s age, the childcare or sessions you are interested in, your preferred start date and information you include in your enquiry.

Please do not include unnecessary medical, safeguarding or other sensitive information in a general website enquiry.

Information collected when visiting the nursery

We may record your name, arrival and departure times, purpose of visit, the person you are visiting, vehicle registration where relevant, and information required for safeguarding, security and emergency purposes. Visitors currently sign in using the nursery’s paper visitor book.

3. How we collect information

We may collect information directly from parents and carers; through registration forms, contracts and introductory forms; during visits, meetings, telephone calls and email correspondence; through our website enquiry form and the Famly platform; from local authorities, health, education or social-care professionals and previous settings; through funding forms; and through records created by nursery staff.

Some information is required so that we can provide childcare, meet legal duties or administer funded places. Other information is optional.

4. Why we use personal information

We use children’s information to provide safe and individualised care, support learning and development, manage attendance and funded childcare, support medical, dietary and SEND needs, safeguard children, manage accidents and medication, communicate with families, support transitions, improve our services and meet Ofsted, EYFS and other legal requirements.

We use parents’ and carers’ information to respond to enquiries, arrange visits, administer nursery places and contracts, communicate about a child’s care, identify authorised collectors, administer payments and funding, respond to concerns or complaints and meet legal and regulatory duties.

Website information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange visits, discuss availability, maintain website security and operation, understand website use where appropriate cookie choices have been made, and keep a record of correspondence.

5. Our lawful bases for using information

Depending on the purpose, we may rely on contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests, consent or vital interests.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before consent was withdrawn.

6. Special-category information

Some information is given extra protection under data-protection law. This can include information about health, disabilities, special educational needs, ethnicity, religion and certain safeguarding matters.

We only use special-category information where we have both an appropriate lawful basis under Article 6 of the UK GDPR and an appropriate condition under Article 9 and, where relevant, the Data Protection Act 2018. Depending on the purpose, this may include explicit consent, vital interests or processing necessary for reasons of substantial public interest. For safeguarding matters, the Data Protection Act 2018 includes a specific condition relating to safeguarding children and individuals at risk.

Where the law requires an Appropriate Policy Document, the nursery will maintain one.

7. Criminal-offence and safeguarding information

We may receive or use information relating to alleged or actual criminal offences where this is necessary as part of safeguarding, legal or regulatory responsibilities. Where Article 10 of the UK GDPR applies, we will only process this information where we have an Article 6 lawful basis and the processing is authorised by UK law, including an applicable Schedule 1 condition under the Data Protection Act 2018.

8. Who we share information with

We only share personal information where there is a lawful and appropriate reason to do so. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • schools or childcare settings a child moves to;
  • the relevant local authority;
  • Ofsted;
  • the Department for Education or organisations administering childcare funding;
  • healthcare professionals;
  • safeguarding partners, children’s services or the police;
  • emergency services;
  • professional advisers, insurers or legal representatives;
  • payment or banking providers;
  • providers of email, cloud storage, website hosting, website enquiry forms, IT or secure data-storage services;
  • Famly;
  • accountants or other business-service providers where they need access to personal information; and
  • other organisations where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect a child or another person.

We do not sell personal information. Information may sometimes be shared without consent, particularly where required by law or where necessary for safeguarding.

9. Service providers and Famly

We use selected service providers to help us operate the nursery. Where an organisation processes personal information on our behalf, we require appropriate data-protection arrangements to be in place.

Famly

The nursery uses Famly as its nursery-management and parent-communication platform. Depending on how the nursery uses the platform, information held in Famly may include child and parent contact details, attendance and absence information, care information such as meals, sleep and nappies, learning observations, photographs and files, messages and other records uploaded by authorised nursery users.

Parents and carers are given access through a secure, invitation-based account. The Wonder Years remains the data controller for information it places in Famly. Famly ApS acts as a data processor when it handles that information on the nursery’s behalf.

Famly’s current Data Processing Agreement states that its main platform hosting is provided through Amazon Web Services in Germany, with encrypted backups in Switzerland. Famly also uses authorised sub-processors, some of which may process limited information outside the UK, EEA or Switzerland for technical support or optional platform features, subject to appropriate transfer safeguards.

The nursery controls how long children’s records are kept in Famly and can export or permanently delete data using Famly’s data-management tools. Famly states that when a nursery’s contract with Famly ends, customer data is returned or deleted within 60 days unless another arrangement or legal requirement applies.

Famly data processing information for families →Famly Data Processing Agreement →

Other service providers

The nursery may also use providers for business email, cloud services, banking and payments, accounting, website hosting and website enquiries. These providers only receive information necessary for the service they provide and are required to handle it in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

The nursery currently uses a paper visitor sign-in book and does not use a third-party visitor-management system.

10. International transfers

Some service providers may store or access personal information outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure that an appropriate legal safeguard applies. This may include UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, an approved data-privacy framework, binding corporate rules or another safeguard permitted by UK law.

Famly’s primary platform hosting is in Germany and encrypted backups are held in Switzerland. Some authorised sub-processors may process limited information in other countries, including the United States, subject to the safeguards described in Famly’s Data Processing Agreement.

12. Photographs and videos

We may take or receive photographs and videos to document learning and development, communicate securely with parents, create nursery displays and records, record events and activities, and for publicity or marketing where separate and appropriate permission has been obtained.

Private sharing through a secure parent platform is treated separately from public use on social media, the nursery website or promotional material. Photographs and videos are kept only for as long as needed for their purpose, subject to any legal or safeguarding reason to retain them.

13. Website cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for it to work securely. If we use non-essential cookies, analytics or third-party content that requires consent, those technologies will not be activated until the appropriate choice has been made.

Further information will be provided through the website’s cookie information and controls where required.

14. Security

We take appropriate organisational and technical measures to protect personal information against accidental or unlawful loss, unauthorised access, improper disclosure, alteration, destruction or misuse.

Measures include secure storage of paper records, access controls and password protection for electronic systems, restricting access to staff who need information for their roles, staff training and confidentiality requirements, secure use of nursery systems and devices, and secure destruction or shredding when records are no longer required.

Staff do not use personal devices to access nursery records as part of normal nursery practice, and access to nursery systems is restricted to authorised users.

15. Personal-data breaches

We have procedures for identifying, investigating and responding to personal-data breaches. Where legally required, we will report a qualifying breach to the Information Commissioner’s Office and inform affected individuals where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

16. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis being used, you may have the right to be informed, request access, request correction or erasure, restrict processing, object to certain uses, receive certain information in a portable format, withdraw consent and challenge certain decisions made solely by automated means.

These rights are not absolute. We may need to retain information to meet legal, safeguarding or regulatory requirements.

To exercise a right, contact Mandy Barker, Nursery Manager at manager@thewonderyearsdaynursery.com.

17. Automated decision-making

We do not currently make decisions about children, parents or carers solely through automated processing where those decisions have legal or similarly significant effects.
