Learning and care

Food and Everyday Care

A nursery day is made up of many small moments, from meals and rest to play, comfort, conversation and everyday routines.

At The Wonder Years, care is shaped around the age and needs of the children in each room. The team works to help children feel comfortable, supported and included throughout the day.

Food prepared at the nursery

Meals and snacks made on the premises

All food and drink is provided by the nursery.

Meals and snacks are prepared and cooked on the premises by the nursery cook.

The nursery caters for allergies and dietary requirements. Parents and carers should share any relevant information with the team before their child starts and keep the nursery updated if anything changes.

If there is anything specific you need to know about menus, ingredients or dietary arrangements, please contact the nursery directly.

Nursery food image placeholder Nursery-prepared food, the kitchen area or a carefully presented mealtime setup

Mealtimes as part of the day

Shared routines and everyday skills

Mealtimes are part of the nursery routine and provide opportunities for children to:

  • become familiar with different foods;
  • develop confidence around eating;
  • practise everyday skills;
  • communicate with adults and other children; and
  • take part in a shared part of the day.

The team supports children according to their age, stage of development and individual needs.

Allergies and dietary requirements

Clear, up-to-date information

The nursery caters for allergies and dietary requirements.

Parents and carers should provide clear, up-to-date information about:

  • diagnosed allergies;
  • dietary requirements;
  • foods that must be avoided;
  • any relevant medical advice; and
  • changes to their child’s needs.

The nursery can then discuss the arrangements needed for the child’s care.

What parents provide

Personal care items

Parents provide their child’s:

  • nappies;
  • wipes;
  • creams; and
  • sunscreen, where these are needed.

Please make sure items are clearly identified and kept up to date.

If there is anything else your child needs as part of their everyday care, speak to the nursery before they start.

Rest and sleep

Space to rest when children need it

Each room has its own sleep mats and blankets for children who need to rest or sleep during the day.

After lunch, an area within the room can be cleared to create a suitable rest space.

Children’s rest needs can vary according to their age, routines and development. The nursery team supports these needs within the arrangements available in each room.

If there is anything specific the team needs to know about your child’s sleep or rest routine, please discuss it with the nursery.

Care that reflects the child

Individual support throughout the day

Children are treated as individuals, and everyday care is adapted around their age, stage of development and personal needs.

This may include support with:

  • settling into the room;
  • eating and drinking;
  • rest and sleep;
  • nappy changing and toileting;
  • comfort and reassurance;
  • communication;
  • transitions between activities; and
  • growing independence.

The team works to build familiarity and consistency so children can feel secure within the nursery day.

Keeping parents informed

Updates through the Famly app

The nursery uses the Famly app to help parents stay connected.

Updates may include information about:

  • meals;
  • sleep;
  • nappies;
  • activities;
  • learning;
  • photographs;
  • video observations; and
  • everyday care.

This gives families a clearer picture of their child’s day and helps support communication between home and nursery.

Everyday routines and independence

Building confidence through daily tasks

As children grow, everyday routines can become valuable opportunities for learning and independence.

The nursery supports children to take part in age-appropriate tasks such as:

  • making choices;
  • washing and drying their hands;
  • managing belongings;
  • helping with simple routines;
  • communicating their needs; and
  • becoming more confident in caring for themselves.

Support is adapted to the child rather than expecting every child to do the same thing at the same stage.

Outdoor time

Regular access to the nursery garden

The nursery’s outdoor space is limited, so rooms use the garden on a rota throughout the day.

This allows different groups of children to spend time outside while keeping the space manageable for their age and stage.

Outdoor time forms part of the nursery day alongside indoor play, activities, meals and rest.

What to tell the nursery

Information that supports consistent care

Please let the nursery know about anything that may affect your child’s care, including:

  • allergies or dietary requirements;
  • sleep and rest routines;
  • creams or personal care items;
  • health information;
  • comfort needs;
  • changes at home that may affect the child; and
  • anything that helps the team understand how best to support them.

Open communication helps the nursery provide care that is consistent, appropriate and responsive.

What happens if my child is unwell?

Speak to the team

If you have questions about food, rest, routines, allergies or everyday care, please contact the nursery.
