Funding and joining

Funding and Joining the Nursery

We understand that choosing childcare involves both practical decisions and finding somewhere that feels right for your child.

This page explains the sessions available at The Wonder Years, the funded childcare options the nursery accepts and what happens when you are ready to arrange a visit and reserve a place.

Childcare sessions

Flexible options for families

The nursery offers flexible childcare to help meet the different needs of families.

Available options include:

  • full-day care;
  • five-hour sessions; and
  • hourly childcare.

The team can talk through the available options with you during your nursery visit.

Tax-Free Childcare and childcare vouchers

Accepted payment support

The nursery accepts:

  • Tax-Free Childcare; and
  • childcare vouchers.

If you are unsure which funding or payment option applies to your family, the nursery team can explain what it accepts and where you may need to check your eligibility.

Funded childcare

Funded places from nine months

The Wonder Years offers funded childcare from nine months old for eligible families.

This includes:

  • Funded childcare for eligible working families from nine months
  • Early Learning for Two
  • Universal funded childcare for three- and four-year-olds
  • Up to 30 funded hours for eligible families

How funded hours can be used

Two usual attendance patterns

Funded hours can be taken in different ways to help them work around family needs.

30 hours a week

Over 38 weeks of the year

22.5 hours a week

Over 50 weeks of the year

Other patterns may also be considered, subject to the nursery’s available sessions.

What’s included

Food, drinks and activities

There are no separate charges for food, drinks or nursery activities.

Find out more about food and everyday care

Included as part of your child’s time at nursery:

  • meals and snacks;
  • drinks;
  • nursery activities and learning experiences.

Parents provide nappies, wipes, creams and sunscreen where these are needed.

Arranging a nursery visit

Come and explore the nursery

Prospective parents are encouraged to telephone or email to make an appointment for a nursery tour.

A visit gives you the opportunity to:

  • look around the nursery;
  • see the rooms and age groups;
  • meet members of the team;
  • discuss your child’s age, routines and needs;
  • talk about the sessions you are looking for;
  • ask about funded childcare; and
  • discuss current and expected availability.
Arrange a nursery tour

Reserving a place

Planning ahead

Once you have visited and decided that you would like your child to attend, the nursery can discuss reserving a place with you.

The Wonder Years does not operate a traditional waiting list. Instead, places are booked using the nursery’s projected availability.

Families are encouraged to visit and reserve their place as early as possible, as bookings are often made around 12 months in advance.

Settling-in image placeholder A calm nursery room, welcoming play area or staff member preparing an activity for a new child

Settling in

Moving at your child’s pace

Every child responds differently to a new environment, so settling in is approached at the child’s own pace.

The first settling-in visit usually lasts around 30 minutes. This gives your child time to meet the staff and begin becoming familiar with the room and nursery environment.

Further visits are arranged after the team has met your child and seen how they respond. These sessions gradually build up to around two hours, with the aim of helping your child feel secure and comfortable before their usual childcare sessions begin.

The nursery offers free settling-in visits to support this process.

What to discuss with the nursery

Information that helps the team

When you contact the team, it is helpful to share:

  • your child’s age;
  • your preferred start date;
  • the days and hours you are looking for;
  • whether you may be eligible for funded childcare;
  • any dietary requirements or allergies; and
  • anything that may help the team understand your child’s routines or support needs.

If there is anything specific you need to know about payment options, funding, sessions or joining the nursery, please contact the team directly.
