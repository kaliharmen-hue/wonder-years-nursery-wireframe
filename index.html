Welcome to The Wonder Years

A nursery where every child is known

Choosing a nursery is a big decision. You want somewhere your child will feel safe, settled and understood, where the team takes the time to know them as an individual.

Care is shaped around each child rather than expecting every child to fit the same routine.

Learning and development

Learning built around your child

Our curriculum follows the Early Years Foundation Stage and is informed by Birth to 5 Matters, supporting children across the seven areas of learning and development.

Children are observed by their key person and the wider team so activities can reflect their interests, progress and next steps.

Settling in

Time to settle at their own pace

The first session usually lasts around 30 minutes. Further visits are arranged around how each child responds, gradually building up to two hours.

The nursery offers as many free settling-in sessions as each child needs.
