What Ofsted said

Children are happy and settled

The report describes children as happy and settled within an inviting nursery. It also notes that parents spoke very positively about the nursery and said their children enjoy attending.

Strengths highlighted in the report

  • children learn and play in a safe environment;
  • staff support language through stories and conversation;
  • children behave well and show kindness and respect;
  • the curriculum covers all seven areas of learning;
  • activities reflect children’s interests and next steps;
  • children receive varied, balanced homemade meals and snacks;
  • staff encourage independence and confidence;
  • settling-in sessions reflect individual children;
  • children and staff form close, caring relationships; and
  • safeguarding arrangements are effective.

Learning, care and development

Understanding each child’s progress

Inspectors found that staff gather useful information from parents when children start nursery.

The team uses observations and assessments to understand children’s progress, identify what they need to learn next and recognise where early or ongoing support may be needed.

  • communication and language;
  • books and storytelling;
  • positive behaviour and independence;
  • healthy food and respectful personal care; and
  • helping children feel safe and secure.

Safeguarding

Effective arrangements

Ofsted found that the nursery’s safeguarding arrangements are effective and described an open, positive safeguarding culture that puts children’s interests first.

Read about safeguarding, SEND and inclusion

Areas for continued improvement

Continuing to strengthen provision

Ofsted identified two areas where the nursery could continue to develop following the inspection.

01

Plan more precisely during transition times so children remain engaged in learning.

02

Strengthen the ways children are encouraged to continue learning at home.
