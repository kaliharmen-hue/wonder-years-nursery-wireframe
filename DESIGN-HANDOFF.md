# The Wonder Years homepage wireframe: design handoff

This prototype is intended to define content hierarchy, page rhythm and responsive behaviour. It is not a finished visual identity.

## Core user journey

The homepage should help a parent quickly establish:

1. whether the nursery serves their child’s age;
2. opening hours, session flexibility and funded childcare;
3. whether the nursery feels safe, caring and credible;
4. how learning, settling in and daily communication work; and
5. how to arrange a visit.

The primary action throughout the page is **Arrange a visit**. Other links should support exploration without competing visually with it.

## Layout

- Keep one clear H1 in the hero and use H2 headings for each major homepage section.
- Retain a maximum content width close to 1180px, while keeping paragraph text to roughly 60 to 70 characters per line.
- Use generous and consistent vertical spacing between sections.
- Alternate image and text sections on larger screens to create rhythm, but keep the logical reading order in the HTML. On mobile, the heading and copy should appear before the supporting image.
- Collapse four-column card grids to two columns on tablet and one column on small screens.
- Do not add decorative elements that make the page feel childish or visually busy. The audience is parents, not children.

## Calls to action

- The principal button style is reserved for arranging a visit and other important next steps.
- Secondary actions should use an outlined button or a clearly styled text link.
- Buttons and links must retain visible keyboard focus states and comfortable touch targets.

## Images

- Use genuine nursery photography wherever possible.
- Stock photography may be used selectively where no suitable real image exists, but it should feel natural and consistent with the nursery.
- Do not add AI-generated children to photographs of the real nursery.
- Give every image an appropriate aspect ratio so space is reserved before it loads.
- Use descriptive alt text where the image conveys information. Use empty alt text for purely decorative images.
- Supply responsive image sizes rather than loading the same large file on every device.

## Accessibility and responsive behaviour

- Preserve semantic landmarks, heading hierarchy and the skip link.
- Ensure colour contrast meets WCAG AA.
- Do not communicate meaning through colour alone.
- Keep the mobile menu keyboard accessible and make it close after a navigation choice.
- Check the page at 200% and 400% zoom, as well as with keyboard-only navigation.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid changing the visual order of content in a way that conflicts with its source order.

## Content and trust

- Keep factual claims specific and supportable.
- Do not imply that places are currently available. Direct families to discuss availability.
- Replace all testimonial placeholders with genuine approved comments.
- Link the Ofsted section directly to the correct report.
- Keep exact fees off the homepage unless the nursery decides to maintain them there.
- Confirm the final curriculum terminology before publication.

## Before launch

- Replace every image placeholder.
- Test all navigation and footer links.
- Add the final privacy notice, cookie information and complaints information.
- Configure the map and any analytics behind appropriate cookie controls.
- Test on current mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.
- Run accessibility, performance and structured-data checks.
- Carry out a brief review with several parents who have not been involved in the project and observe whether they can find ages, opening times, funding and the visit action quickly.
