Arrange a nursery tour

Plan your visit

Prospective parents are encouraged to telephone or email to make an appointment before visiting.

During the tour, the team can show you around the nursery, explain how the rooms are organised and discuss:

  • your child’s age and needs;
  • the days or sessions you require;
  • your preferred start date;
  • funded childcare where relevant;
  • current and expected availability; and
  • the settling-in process.

Availability varies according to the child’s age, the sessions required and the requested start date.

The nursery does not operate a traditional waiting list. Places are reserved based on projected availability, and families are encouraged to visit and book early because places are often reserved around 12 months in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Questions parents often ask

What ages of children do you accept?

The nursery provides childcare from six weeks to 11 years old.

School-age children can attend during the school holidays up to Year 6, subject to availability.

Do you offer school pickups or after-school care?

No. The nursery does not currently collect children from school or provide after-school care that requires school pickup.

What are your opening hours?

The nursery is open Monday to Friday from 7.30am to 6pm.

It is open throughout the year apart from bank holidays, Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve.

What happens if my child is unwell?

Read our guidance about when children should stay at home, what happens if a child becomes unwell at nursery and common exclusion periods.

Read illness and exclusion guidance
What types of sessions are available?

The nursery offers full-day care, five-hour sessions and hourly childcare.

All sessions are subject to availability.

How do I arrange a visit?

Telephone or email the nursery to make an appointment.

During your visit, the team will show you around and discuss your child, the sessions you need and current availability.

Do you have a waiting list?

The nursery does not operate a traditional waiting list.

Places are booked using projected availability, so families can reserve a place in advance once they have decided they would like their child to attend.

How far ahead should I reserve a place?

Families are encouraged to visit and reserve their place as early as possible. The nursery often accepts bookings around 12 months in advance.

What happens when my child starts settling in?

The first settling-in visit usually lasts around 30 minutes. This gives your child an opportunity to meet the staff and begin becoming familiar with the environment.

Further visits are then arranged according to how your child responds, gradually building up to two hours.

The nursery offers as many free settling-in sessions as your child needs.

Do you offer funded childcare?

Yes. The nursery offers funded childcare from nine months for eligible families.

This includes Early Learning for Two places, universal funded childcare for three- and four-year-olds and up to 30 funded hours for eligible families.

Funded hours can usually be taken as 30 hours a week over 38 weeks or 22.5 hours a week over 50 weeks. Other patterns may be available, subject to availability.

Do you accept Tax-Free Childcare and childcare vouchers?

Yes. The nursery accepts Tax-Free Childcare and childcare vouchers.

Are food and activities included?

Yes. Food, drinks and nursery activities are provided without a separate additional charge.

Parents provide their child’s nappies, wipes, creams and sunscreen where needed.

Do you cater for allergies and dietary requirements?

Yes. Food is prepared and cooked on the premises by the nursery cook, and allergies and dietary requirements are catered for.

Is parking available?

Yes. There is a small car park for parents, visitors and staff.

Use WS10 9DH, Reservoir Passage for the car park rather than the nursery’s main postal postcode.

Can I ask about additional needs before my child starts?

Yes. Parents can discuss their child’s individual needs with the nursery before starting.

Kirsty Burke is the nursery SENDCO, and support is considered according to each child’s individual needs and way of learning.
