Our rooms

A space for every stage

Our separate rooms give babies, toddlers and preschool children an environment suited to their age, stage of development and individual needs.

We also welcome school-age children during the school holidays, up to Year 6, subject to availability.

Explore our rooms

Six weeks to around two years

Ladybirds and Dragonflies

Ladybirds and Dragonflies form the nursery’s independent baby unit, providing a separate environment for babies and younger toddlers.

At this stage, children need care that responds to their individual routines, comfort and development. The room team gets to know each child and shapes their care around their needs rather than expecting every child to follow exactly the same pattern.

The environment and activities are suited to younger children as they begin exploring, communicating, moving and building secure relationships with the adults caring for them.

Approximately two to three years

Bumble Bees and Caterpillars

Bumble Bees and Caterpillars support children through a stage of rapid growth in language, confidence, movement and independence.

Bumble Bees generally accommodates the younger two-year-olds and Caterpillars the older children. There is no rigid move-up age because room placement also depends on the needs of the children and the staffing ratios in place at the time.

Activities and support are shaped around the children in each room, giving them opportunities to explore, communicate, make choices and become increasingly confident in everyday routines.

Three years and over

Grasshoppers

Grasshoppers is the nursery’s room for children aged three and over.

As children grow, the room offers new challenges and learning opportunities while continuing to respond to their individual interests and next steps.

The team also helps children prepare for the transition to school. During their final term, staff gather information about each child’s new school, talk with them about the change and, where possible, invite new teachers into the nursery to meet the children in a familiar environment.

School holidays

Holiday care up to Year 6

The nursery also accepts school-age children during the school holidays, up to Year 6.

Places are subject to availability, so families should contact the nursery to discuss the dates and sessions they require.

Rest and daily routines

Space to rest when children need it

Each room has its own sleep mats and blankets. After lunch, an area within the room can be cleared to provide space for children who need to sleep or rest.

Daily care is adapted to the age and needs of the children in the room, helping younger children and those who still need daytime sleep remain comfortable throughout the day.

Outdoor time

Regular access to the nursery garden

As the nursery’s outdoor space is limited, the rooms use the garden on a rota throughout the day.

This enables different groups of children to spend time outside while keeping the space manageable for their age and stage.
